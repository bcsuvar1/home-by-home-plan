The asset management squeeze

Social housing asset managers face unprecedented pressure to provide safe homes while navigating rapid cost inflation, shrinking budgets and complex decarbonisation targets.

You are expected to meet the Decent Homes Standard and Awaab's Law, but your data is often siloed and difficult to act on.

In one London borough's stock, reactive repairs rose by in two years.

An analysis layer, not another database

You don't need another database to manage. You need an analysis layer that makes your existing data work for you.

The Home by Home plan ingests your stock condition surveys, repair histories, EPC data and tenant satisfaction scores.

It sits on top of your current asset management system, cleans anomalies, and gives your teams real, easy-to-interpret outputs.

Level 1

Strategic investment planning

Move from reactive spending to proactive capital allocation.

We set rules-based criteria to decide exactly which properties need which works, from window replacements to roof repairs.

Red: urgent works, where a poor EPC combines with frequent repairs.

That lets you define short, medium and long-term funding programmes…

Amber: medium-term programme.

…aligned with your strategic priorities and available budgets.

Green: long-term, planned cycle.

Level 2

Preventive maintenance

Reduce expensive reactive callouts by understanding the root causes of failure.

We use AI to analyse tens of thousands of historical repair logs, categorising the underlying issues to build a real-life picture of your homes.

The outputs give clear summaries of recurring problems at both the asset group level and the individual property level.

Each home gets a Property Passport: its repair history, its energy rating and the risks that follow from them.

Level 3

Evaluation & tracking impact

Prove the return on your interventions.

Our analysis is not a static report. It is an iterative, long-term process.

As upgrades are completed and new data flows in, the plan updates itself, so you can track the real impact of your capital spending and see which interventions were most cost-effective.
